Welcome!

I am an Associate Professor of Finance at the HKU Business School, University of Hong Kong.

My primary research interests are household finance, investments, financial institutions, real estate, and sustainability.

My work has appeared in the Journal of Financial Economics, Journal of Accounting and Economics, Management Science, Review of Economics and Statistics, Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis, and Review of Finance. I am an Associate Editor of the Journal of Business Research.
